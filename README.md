# Cuisine Court

Settle dinner once and for all. Choose between two cuisines at a time; the court builds a ranking from your rulings and objects the moment your taste contradicts itself.

**Play it here: https://kleinlennart.github.io/cuisine-court/**

## How it works

- Two cuisines appear. Tap the one you'd rather eat.
- Cross-examination rounds test whether your earlier picks hold up.
- Rule A over B, B over C, then C over A? Objection. Stand by it or overrule yourself.
- At the end you get a verdict, a credibility score, and a summary to paste into the group chat.

Pick **Gut feeling** for quick play, or **Debate mode** for a scenario prompt and a 30-second timer on every matchup.

## Running locally

It's a single self-contained page. Progress is saved in your browser's local storage.
